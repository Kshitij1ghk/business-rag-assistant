# Employee Benefits

## Health insurance
Acme Corp pays 100 percent of the health insurance premium for the employee and 70 percent for dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

## Learning and development
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses, books and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Retirement plan
The company matches employee retirement contributions up to 5 percent of salary. Matching contributions vest after 2 years of service.

## Wellness
Employees get a monthly gym reimbursement of up to 40 USD and access to a confidential employee assistance programme.
