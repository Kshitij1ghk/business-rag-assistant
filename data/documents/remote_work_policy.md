# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period may work remotely.
Roles that require physical presence, such as facilities and front-desk staff, are not eligible.

## Schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Every team must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays for collaboration days.

## Equipment
Acme Corp provides a laptop and a monitor to every remote employee. A one-time home office allowance of 300 USD is available for a desk or chair.

## Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Remote employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours.
