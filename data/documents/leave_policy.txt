Acme Corp Leave Policy

Annual leave: Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year.
Sick leave: Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year.
Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Parental leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

Leave requests: Annual leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days.
Sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
